# BataMart Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 6, 2026**

This policy explains what information BataMart ("we", "us", "the App") collects from
users of the BataMart mobile app and website (batamart.com), why we collect it, and how
it is handled. BataMart is a campus marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, and delivery
riders at Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK), with support for additional universities
as they are added.

By creating an account or using BataMart, you agree to the collection and use of
information as described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information**: name, email address, phone number, password (stored hashed,
never in plain text), matriculation number (optional), and profile photo (optional).

**Delivery information**: hostel name, room number, and delivery landmark/zone, used to
calculate delivery fees and route orders to riders.

**Order and marketplace activity**: products listed or purchased, order history,
reviews and ratings you leave or receive, messages sent through the dispute-resolution
system, and referral codes you use or share.

**Payment information**: BataMart does not store your card or bank details. Payments
are processed by **Flutterwave**, a licensed payment processor, which handles all card
and transfer data directly. BataMart only receives confirmation that a payment
succeeded and the transaction reference. Wallet withdrawals are paid out via
Flutterwave's transfer service to the bank account you provide at withdrawal time.

**Rider verification**: riders must submit a government-issued ID document during
registration, used solely to verify identity before approval. Rider location is shared
with the buyer **only for the duration of an active delivery** they are assigned to, so
the buyer can track their order.

**Withdrawal PIN**: sellers set a PIN to authorize wallet withdrawals. On devices that
support it, the app may additionally ask for your fingerprint or face unlock before a
withdrawal — this biometric check happens entirely on your device via your device's own
operating system and is never transmitted to or stored by BataMart.

**Automatically collected information**: basic device and diagnostic information (app
version, device type, crash logs) used to keep the app working correctly.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and maintain your account and let you use buyer, seller, or rider features
- To process orders, calculate delivery fees, and coordinate deliveries between buyers,
  sellers, and riders
- To process payments and withdrawals through Flutterwave
- To send you one-time verification codes, order updates, and account notifications by
  email
- To verify rider identity before approving a rider account
- To resolve disputes, investigate reports, and enforce our terms (including account
  suspension for policy violations)
- To improve the reliability and performance of the App

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Third Parties We Share Data With

We share the minimum data necessary with the following service providers to operate
BataMart:

| Provider | Purpose | Data shared |
|---|---|---|
| Flutterwave | Payment processing and wallet withdrawals | Payment amount, order reference; card/bank details are entered directly with Flutterwave, not through BataMart |
| Resend | Sending verification-code and account emails | Your email address |
| Google | "Continue with Google" sign-in | Basic profile info (name, email) Google shares when you sign in |
| Cloud infrastructure providers | Hosting our database and backend servers | All account and order data, stored securely |

Each of these providers is contractually restricted to using your data only to provide
their service to us.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and for a
reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, tax, and dispute-resolution
obligations. You can request deletion of your account at any time (see Section 6).

## 5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted
connections (HTTPS) between the app and our servers, hashed password storage, and
access controls on our database. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your
data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

## 6. Your Rights

You can:

- Access and update most of your account information directly in the app (Profile
  settings)
- Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- Request that we delete your account and associated personal data, subject to
  retention we're legally required to keep (e.g. transaction records)
- Withdraw consent for optional features (such as push notifications) at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details in Section 9.

## 7. Children's Privacy

BataMart is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will
notify users through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this
page reflects the most recent revision.
